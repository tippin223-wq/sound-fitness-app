# Unreal Learning Notes

Use this folder to turn Unreal Engine videos, docs, experiments, and ideas into reusable build knowledge.

## Workflow

1. Add the video link to `source-log.md`.
2. Add a short transcript, caption export, or your rough notes under `video-notes/`.
3. Extract the useful steps into `build-notes/`.
4. Convert repeatable work into checklists under `checklists/`.

## Copyright Note

Do not keep full copied YouTube transcripts here unless you own the content or have permission. Prefer short summaries, timestamps, concepts, and your own step-by-step notes.
