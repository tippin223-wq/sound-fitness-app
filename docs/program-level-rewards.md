# Program level rewards

Status: draft reward definitions and authored cosmetic design briefs. Not published, not claimable, and not connected to account balances. No inventory items or member data have been created.

## Rules

- One reward per level, beginning at level 1; program progress begins at level 0.
- Every fifth level replaces the ordinary currency reward with one specific cosmetic.
- Cosmetic IDs, names, and design briefs are globally distinct across tracks. No recycled items or automatic color-only substitutions.
- Ordinary currency types are randomly chosen once when authoring a program, at fixed amounts. Persist the whole track in trusted storage; do not generate a new draw when displaying, claiming, or enrolling. All members on a published program version receive its same frozen track.
- A saved track cannot change its title, total levels, or allocated rewards through the allocator. Add a program version for a revised schedule. Retain retired tracks so their cosmetics cannot be reassigned.
- The current 60-design catalog has a hard capacity. Exhaustion blocks new allocations until more genuinely distinct designs are authored.
- Existing weekly plans do not yet identify full multiweek programs. The schedules below illustrate allocation and are not enrolled member programs.

| Reward | Amount | Selection weight |
|---|---:|---:|

Emeralds remain outside the common pool because their established purpose is redeeming real services. A separate limited emerald award policy is still required. Currency prizes never advance program progress.

## Remaining implementation before publication

1. Define stable full-program IDs/versions and freeze actual workout/support schedules after Profile and Goals are complete.
2. Produce and review each wearable/background asset from its design brief; add asset references, garment/headgear fitting, previews, and inventory equip support. These briefs are not finished 3D assets.
3. Store published tracks with database uniqueness for program/version/level and cosmetic assignment. Allocate new tracks transactionally; concurrent authoring cannot reserve the same item twice.
4. Award from server-verified program progress with a unique account/program-version/level ledger entry. Claiming twice or editing logs must not award twice. Grant milestone cosmetics directly to inventory, rather than requiring coins to buy an earned prize.
5. Preserve existing wallet and weekly-spin services for currency fulfillment; award currency and mark the level claimed atomically. No client-side wallet mutation.
6. Feed the member level popup and next-reward preview from that published track and verified progress. Do not show these example schedules as live user data.
