## Purpose

This vault exists to help Codex stay accurate across longer app work:

- preserve product intent and user preferences
- keep route and feature context easy to find
- track screenshot annotations and completed edits
- record decisions that should influence future changes

## Screenshot Review Workflow

- Put new marked-up screenshots in `screenshots/inbox`.
- Say `start edits`.
- Codex reviews every unhandled screenshot in that folder.
- Codex makes the requested app edits.
- Codex moves handled screenshots to `screenshots/reviewed`.
- Codex summarizes decisions and completed edits in [[Review Log]].
